Cozumel

Island Endemics

Cozumel Vireo · Cozumel Emerald · Cozumel Wren

Cozumel is an island shaped by isolation, where the forest holds species found nowhere else. Explore its jungle, coast and mangroves in search of the Cozumel Vireo, Cozumel Emerald and Cozumel Wren.

With Elvis, the experience goes beyond simply looking for birds — reading the habitat, listening for movement and knowing where the island reveals its hidden life.

Depending on the route, the morning may include stops such as Punta Sur and El Cedral, among other spots across the island.

02

Yucatán

Flamingos & Wetlands

Phoenicopterus ruber

Across the wetlands of the Yucatán Peninsula, water, mangroves and open skies create a landscape alive with birdlife. The American Flamingo is one of its defining presences, while the wetlands reveal a constantly changing world of movement, color and sound.

Exploring places including Río Lagartos, Las Coloradas, Celestún and Chuburná, depending on the route.

Chiapas

The Lacandon Jungle

Jungle · Birding · Wildlife

Deep in the Lacandon Jungle, the landscape becomes denser, wilder and more immersive. Forest, rivers and hidden clearings create an extraordinary setting for exploring the birdlife and wildlife of southern Mexico.

A private, multi-day experience for those who want more time in the field and the freedom to let the journey unfold at its own pace.

Before You Go

What to Expect

Wildlife does not follow a schedule.

Sightings depend on season, weather, habitat and the behavior of each species. Every outing is an invitation to slow down, pay attention and experience the field as it unfolds.
