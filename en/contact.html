02 — Before You Go

What to expect & what to bring.

Practical essentials to ensure you enjoy every moment in the field.

01

Start Early

Early morning hours are typically the most active and rewarding for birding and wildlife encounters before the midday heat.

02

Dress Comfortably

Wear lightweight, breathable clothing in natural or muted tones, paired with comfortable closed-toe walking shoes or boots.

03

Bring Your Optics

Binoculars and cameras are warmly welcome. If you need a pair of binoculars, let Elvis know in advance so we can arrange one.

04

Bring the Essentials

A refillable water bottle, a hat, reef-friendly sun protection, and eco-friendly insect repellent to protect fragile habitats.

03 — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need previous birding experience?

Not at all. Every tour is private and customized to your pace. Whether you are picking up binoculars for the first time or you are a seasoned birder pursuing specific lifers, Elvis tailors the outing to your background and curiosity.

Can I request specific birds or wildlife?

Absolutely. If you have specific target species (such as the Cozumel Emerald, Cozumel Vireo, Cozumel Wren, or Yucatán specialties), let Elvis know when you reach out so he can optimize the locations and timing.

Is photography allowed?

Yes, warmly encouraged! Elvis is a professional wildlife photographer and can help you position for the best natural light, clean angles, and ethical photo encounters without stressing wildlife.

Where do we meet?

For Cozumel tours, we can meet you directly at the ferry pier, your hotel lobby, or a convenient central landmark. For Yucatán and Chiapas expeditions, meeting details are coordinated directly with you.

What if the weather is not favorable?

Light tropical sprinkles rarely stop the birds, but safety and enjoyment always come first. In case of heavy rain or severe weather, we will work together to reschedule your tour or adjust the itinerary.

How do I book?

Booking is direct and personal. Send Elvis a message on WhatsApp with your desired destination and dates. Once agreed, your tour is locked in. Payment is made in cash on the day of the tour.
