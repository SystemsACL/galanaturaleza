The Person Behind the Experience

"He knows where to look, what to listen for, and how to bring you closer to the wild."

Elvis Jiménez

Nature Guide · Birdwatcher · Wildlife Photographer

Elvis Jiménez is a nature guide, birdwatcher, and wildlife photographer. His passion has taken him across the diverse ecosystems of Cozumel, Yucatán, and Chiapas, where he has developed a deep knowledge of birds and wildlife.

With an expert yet personal approach, Elvis turns every journey into an authentic opportunity to discover nature.

Species & Habitats

Birds & Nature

From coastal mangroves and wetlands to tropical forests and Caribbean reefs, Mexico is home to an extraordinary diversity of ecosystems and wildlife. At Gala Naturaleza, we explore these landscapes with a special focus on birds, endemic species, and the wildlife that inhabits them, always with a deep respect for the natural places that make these encounters possible.
